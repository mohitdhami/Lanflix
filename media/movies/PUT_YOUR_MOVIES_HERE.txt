Put your movies here. Two ways:

1) Folder per movie (recommended — supports poster + subtitles + metadata):
   media/movies/Dune Part Two (2024)/Dune Part Two (2024).mp4
   media/movies/Dune Part Two (2024)/poster.jpg
   media/movies/Dune Part Two (2024)/backdrop.jpg
   media/movies/Dune Part Two (2024)/Dune Part Two (2024).vtt   (subtitles, optional)
   media/movies/Dune Part Two (2024)/metadata.json              (optional, see example below)

2) Loose file:
   media/movies/Avatar (2009).mp4

Then open LanFlix → CRM → Rescan media.

TIP: use .mp4 (H.264) — it plays directly in every browser/TV. .mkv usually does NOT play in browsers.
