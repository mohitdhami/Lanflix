Put your shows here — one folder per show, episodes named with season/episode:

   media/series/Breaking Bad/S01E01 Pilot.mp4
   media/series/Breaking Bad/S01E02 Cats in the Bag.mp4
   media/series/Breaking Bad/poster.jpg        (optional)
   media/series/Breaking Bad/Season 2/S02E01.mp4   (subfolders are fine too)

Accepted episode names: S01E01, s1e2, 1x03 …

Then open LanFlix → CRM → Rescan media.

TIP: use .mp4 (H.264) — it plays directly in every browser/TV. .mkv usually does NOT play in browsers.
